# In-flight ideas

Ideas under consideration for Cities of Progress, not yet decided. Each entry is a question, not a commitment.

## A northstar

The repository has no northstar (ruled 2026-11-18: neither a northstar nor its HTML twin for now). Three intents emerged whilst the requirements were settled, and are noted here as the material for one, should it be wanted later:

- **Everything progresses.** The ages, and with them the economies, the sectors of a civilization, and what a player must attend to, move from gathering by hand towards production and automation.
- **Nobody sits idle.** It is a game played together as allies: a player who loses everything receives a founder and builds again whilst an ally still stands.
- **It follows history.** The ages run from Stone to AI, the civilizations are real ones, and the economies follow how things were made (bronze from copper and tin, power from oil).

An HTML twin, if one is made, would wear the game's own look, which waits for the art style guide.

## Renovation

Engineers could turn a building into another kind in place, the player paying the new kind's cost less half the old one's, over the new kind's construction time. The question is footprints: most kinds differ in size, so a conversion would have to grow or shrink the building where it stands, which needs a placement rule of its own. Until then, demolition (requirement 28) and rebuilding reach the same result for half the old building's cost. Raised 2026-11-18.
